# Product Requirements Document
## Robust UART Sensor Logger

**Version:** 1.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-12

---

## 1. Purpose

This device is a field logger for UART-based sensors. Its purpose is to:

- capture UART data reliably from multiple sensors
- timestamp external SYNC inputs and generated trigger outputs against one shared device clock
- persist all required records to SD card
- preserve enough information for offboard parsing, correlation, and fault analysis

The device is a logger first. Features that are not required for reliable capture, timing, and SD logging are out of scope for this revision.

---

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Multi-port UART data capture
- Configurable per-port UART settings
- Raw UART payload logging
- Optional built-in framing for known protocols
- SYNC input capture
- Trigger output generation
- Single free-running device clock
- Structured binary logging to SD card
- Human-readable status logging
- Startup configuration from SD card
- Local session control

### Out of Scope

- Ethernet or Wi-Fi data streaming
- HTTP API or other remote control interfaces
- NTRIP client or RTCM forwarding
- In-device GNSS, RTC, or network clock discipline
- Runtime-loaded parser plugins
- Offboard correlation tooling

Out-of-scope items shall not be required for compliance with this PRD.

---

## 3. Definitions

- **Device clock:** The monotonic microsecond-resolution clock maintained by the logger and used for all timestamps in device logs.
- **Session:** The period from session start to session stop during which records are written to a session log.
- **Raw capture mode:** A port mode in which received UART bytes are logged without requiring packet framing or protocol parsing.
- **Framed record:** A UART record annotated with packet boundaries detected by a built-in framing function.
- **SYNC input:** A digital timing input whose edges are timestamped by the device.
- **Trigger output:** A digital timing output generated by the device at a configured rate and pulse width.
- **Data loss event:** Any condition in which received UART bytes or required log records cannot be preserved completely.

The terms **shall**, **shall not**, and **must** indicate mandatory requirements. The term **may** indicates non-required behavior.

---

## 4. Product-Level Requirements

- **REQ-PROD-01:** The device shall prioritize UART capture, timing capture, and SD logging over all other functions.
- **REQ-PROD-02:** The SD card log files shall be the system of record for all captured data and device events.
- **REQ-PROD-03:** The device shall preserve raw UART payload data independently of any optional framing or parsing features.
- **REQ-PROD-04:** The device shall log only information that it directly measures or derives explicitly as metadata.
- **REQ-PROD-05:** The device shall remain operable without network connectivity.

---

## 5. Physical Interfaces

### Sensor Ports

- **REQ-PORT-01:** The device shall provide at least 4 sensor ports.
- **REQ-PORT-02:** Each sensor port shall provide the following signals: `VCC`, `GND`, `RX`, `TX`, and `SYNC`.
- **REQ-PORT-03:** `RX` shall mean hub receive from sensor transmit.
- **REQ-PORT-04:** `TX` shall mean hub transmit to sensor receive.
- **REQ-PORT-05:** The `SYNC` signal on each port shall be configurable as either input or output on a per-port basis.
- **REQ-PORT-06:** Ports need not be identical in active role during a session; the firmware shall permit different configured roles per port.

### Power

- **REQ-PORT-07:** Each port shall provide a 3.3 V sensor supply.
- **REQ-PORT-08:** The per-port current limit and total available sensor power budget shall be defined by the hardware design and documented for users.

---

## 6. UART Capture Requirements

- **REQ-UART-01:** Each port shall support asynchronous UART operation with configurable baud rate from 9600 to 921600 bps.
- **REQ-UART-02:** Each port shall support 7 or 8 data bits.
- **REQ-UART-03:** Each port shall support parity modes `none`, `even`, and `odd`.
- **REQ-UART-04:** Each port shall support 1 or 2 stop bits.
- **REQ-UART-05:** Incoming UART bytes shall be captured without software polling.
- **REQ-UART-06:** UART reception shall not be blocked by SD writes, status logging, or optional framing logic.
- **REQ-UART-07:** Each port shall provide buffering sufficient to retain at least 500 ms of incoming data at its configured baud rate.
- **REQ-UART-08:** The device shall support a raw capture mode on every port.
- **REQ-UART-09:** In raw capture mode, the device shall log received UART payload bytes even when no framer or parser is assigned.
- **REQ-UART-10:** The device may provide built-in framing support for known protocols such as NMEA 0183 and UBX.
- **REQ-UART-11:** Built-in framing, if enabled, shall be optional and shall not be required for data preservation.
- **REQ-UART-12:** The device shall detect UART receive overflow conditions.
- **REQ-UART-13:** For each detected UART receive overflow condition, the device shall increment a per-port loss counter and log an error or status record identifying the affected port.
- **REQ-UART-14:** If bytes are lost on a port, the log shall contain enough information to determine that a loss event occurred, even if the exact number of lost bytes cannot be measured.

---

## 7. Synchronization Requirements

### SYNC Input

- **REQ-SYNC-01:** Any port configured for SYNC input shall capture timestamps for rising edges, falling edges, or both, according to configuration.
- **REQ-SYNC-02:** SYNC edge capture shall not rely on software polling.
- **REQ-SYNC-03:** The timestamp precision of captured SYNC edges shall be no worse than 1 us relative to the device clock.
- **REQ-SYNC-04:** Each captured SYNC edge shall be logged as an independent record.
- **REQ-SYNC-05:** Each SYNC edge record shall contain at least the port index, edge polarity, and device-clock timestamp.

### Packet-to-SYNC Metadata

- **REQ-SYNC-06:** The device may associate framed UART records with a SYNC sequence identifier or equivalent metadata.
- **REQ-SYNC-07:** Any packet-to-SYNC association shall be represented in the log as metadata, not as a measured packet timestamp.
- **REQ-SYNC-08:** The absence of packet-to-SYNC association metadata shall not prevent compliant raw capture logging.

---

## 8. Trigger Output Requirements

- **REQ-TRIG-01:** Any port configured for trigger output shall generate pulses at a configurable rate up to 2 kHz.
- **REQ-TRIG-02:** Trigger pulse width shall be configurable.
- **REQ-TRIG-03:** A port shall not operate as both SYNC input and trigger output at the same time.
- **REQ-TRIG-04:** Trigger generation jitter shall be no worse than 5 us under all supported operating conditions, including concurrent UART capture and SD logging.
- **REQ-TRIG-05:** Each generated trigger pulse shall produce a logged trigger record.
- **REQ-TRIG-06:** Each trigger record shall contain at least the port index and device-clock timestamp.

---

## 9. Timing Requirements

- **REQ-CLK-01:** The device shall maintain a single monotonic clock with 1 us resolution.
- **REQ-CLK-02:** All timestamps written to the binary session log shall be derived from the device clock.
- **REQ-CLK-03:** The device clock shall be free-running during a session.
- **REQ-CLK-04:** GNSS time, RTC time, and network time shall not adjust, discipline, redefine, or reset the device clock during a session.
- **REQ-CLK-05:** The session log shall store only device-clock timestamps.
- **REQ-CLK-06:** Any correlation between the device clock and absolute time references shall be performed offboard.

---

## 10. Logging Requirements

### Binary Session Log

- **REQ-LOG-01:** The device shall write a structured binary log to the SD card for each session.
- **REQ-LOG-02:** The binary log shall be the authoritative capture artifact for the session.
- **REQ-LOG-03:** The binary log shall be versioned.
- **REQ-LOG-04:** The binary log format shall be documented sufficiently for an external reader to be implemented without access to firmware source code.
- **REQ-LOG-05:** The binary log shall contain session lifecycle records.
- **REQ-LOG-06:** The binary log shall contain UART data records.
- **REQ-LOG-07:** The binary log shall contain SYNC edge records.
- **REQ-LOG-08:** The binary log shall contain trigger records when trigger output is enabled.
- **REQ-LOG-09:** The binary log shall contain device error and status records relevant to capture integrity.

### Human-Readable Status Log

- **REQ-LOG-10:** The device shall write a separate human-readable status log to the SD card.
- **REQ-LOG-11:** The status log shall include major session events, configuration errors, storage faults, and data-loss indications.

### File Management and Recovery

- **REQ-LOG-12:** Session filenames shall be unique across power cycles.
- **REQ-LOG-13:** The device shall preserve all fully written binary log records across unclean power loss.
- **REQ-LOG-14:** The binary log shall allow detection of an uncleanly terminated session.

---

## 11. Storage Performance and Failure Handling

- **REQ-SD-01:** SD card writes shall not stall UART reception, SYNC capture, or trigger generation.
- **REQ-SD-02:** The device shall use in-memory buffering between the capture path and SD writes.
- **REQ-SD-03:** If SD write throughput falls behind incoming data, the device shall continue logging until available in-memory retention is exhausted.
- **REQ-SD-04:** If in-memory retention is exhausted, the device shall log an explicit data loss event.
- **REQ-SD-05:** The device shall not silently discard required records.
- **REQ-SD-06:** If SD I/O fails during a session, the device shall record the fault in the available local status path and shall stop claiming healthy persistent logging.

---

## 12. Configuration and Operation Requirements

### Configuration

- **REQ-CFG-01:** The device shall read configuration from a structured file on the SD card at startup.
- **REQ-CFG-02:** The configuration file shall support, at minimum, per-port UART settings.
- **REQ-CFG-03:** The configuration file shall support, at minimum, per-port capture mode selection.
- **REQ-CFG-04:** The configuration file shall support, at minimum, per-port SYNC direction selection.
- **REQ-CFG-05:** The configuration file shall support, at minimum, trigger rate and trigger pulse width configuration for ports used as trigger outputs.
- **REQ-CFG-06:** The device shall validate configuration before starting a session.
- **REQ-CFG-07:** Invalid configuration shall prevent session start.
- **REQ-CFG-08:** Invalid configuration shall be reported through a local status mechanism.

### Session Control

- **REQ-OPS-01:** The device shall support a local mechanism to start a session.
- **REQ-OPS-02:** The device shall support a local mechanism to stop a session.
- **REQ-OPS-03:** The local session-control mechanism may be a boot policy, a physical control, a serial console command, or a combination of these.
- **REQ-OPS-04:** Network connectivity shall not be required to start, stop, or complete a session.

---

## 13. Non-Functional Requirements

- **REQ-NF-01:** UART reception, SYNC capture, and trigger generation shall be isolated from optional framing logic and non-critical status handling.
- **REQ-NF-02:** All detected data-loss conditions shall be surfaced explicitly in logs or local status output.
- **REQ-NF-03:** Core modules for UART capture, timing, framing, and logging shall be unit-testable in isolation.
- **REQ-NF-04:** After a firmware reset or restart, prior session data already written to SD card shall remain preserved.
- **REQ-NF-05:** The design shall prefer simple, testable mechanisms over optional feature breadth.

---

## 14. Deferred Features

The following features are intentionally deferred and are not required by this PRD:

- Ethernet streaming
- Wi-Fi streaming
- Remote HTTP control
- NTRIP client
- RTCM forwarding
- Remote configuration update workflow
- In-device absolute-time discipline
- Runtime-loaded parser plugin support
